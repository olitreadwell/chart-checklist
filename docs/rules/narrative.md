# N. Narrative and context

The title, note, and surrounding text state the finding and the limits of the data.

A rule passes, fails, or does not apply. Record `n/a` with a reason. A blank result counts as a failure.

<a id="n1"></a>
## N1. State the finding in the title

**Tier:** critical

**Check:** The title states the finding, not the variable name.

**Fix:** Rewrite the title as a sentence with the subject and the change. Keep it under 15 words.

**Check with:** manual

**Evidence:** [Chartability](https://chartability.fizz.studio/) · [Evergreen Data: Data Visualization Checklist](https://stephanieevergreen.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Data-Viz-Checklist-2024.pdf) · [ONS: Chart text](https://service-manual.ons.gov.uk/data-visualisation/guidance/chart-text)

**Related:** D1, A2

<a id="n2"></a>
## N2. Explain what the chart is for

**Tier:** critical

**Check:** A short note explains what the chart is for and how to read it, in plain language.

**Fix:** Add two or three sentences beside the chart. Name the comparison and the one thing to notice.

**Check with:** manual

**Evidence:** [Chartability](https://chartability.fizz.studio/) · [USWDS: Data visualizations](https://designsystem.digital.gov/components/data-visualizations/)

**Related:** A9, N5

<a id="n3"></a>
## N3. Keep chart text at grade 9 or lower

**Tier:** critical

**Check:** Chart text reads at grade 9 or lower on a Flesch-Kincaid check.

**Fix:** Shorten sentences, remove jargon, and split long labels. Re-run the readability check.

**Check with:** manual, tool

**Evidence:** [Chartability](https://chartability.fizz.studio/) · Chart Checklist house rule

**Related:** N2

<a id="n4"></a>
## N4. Do not imply causation without support

**Tier:** required

**Check:** No causal claim appears unless the source supports it.

**Fix:** Replace causal verbs with neutral ones. State the confounder or drop the claim.

**Check with:** manual

**Evidence:** Chart Checklist house rule · [ONS Service Manual: Data visualisation](https://service-manual.ons.gov.uk/data-visualisation)

**Related:** E8, S5

<a id="n5"></a>
## N5. Answer the question the page asks

**Tier:** required

**Check:** The chart answers the question the page asks.

**Fix:** Re-read the page heading and the chart. Remove the chart or change the measure if they answer different questions.

**Check with:** manual

**Evidence:** Chart Checklist house rule · [USWDS: Data visualizations](https://designsystem.digital.gov/components/data-visualizations/)

**Related:** N1, N2
